How to use this site
• Use this site to find locations and times to see Golden Gate Henge (GGH) for Fall 2026 and Spring 2027.
• Drag the slider to change the date.
• The gold band shows valid areas to view GGH on a given date.
• Red and blue pins show selected viewing locations on- and off-campus and are listed in the dropdown menu to the right (desktop only).
• Click any pin to get sunset time, GGH dates and travel options. GGH dates are approximate.
• Use the search bar to search for pins, addresses, or coordinates, then hit Enter.
• Click “Today” to go to today’s date.
• Click on the tags in the top left to filter pins by on- and off-campus or mode of travel. Select multiple tags to match pins in those groups.
• Click “Filter by Date” to see only valid pins for a given date.
• Click anywhere on the map to drop a pin and get the sunset time.
• Click “Reset” to reset the search and map filters.
• Happy sunset hunting! We do not encourage trespassing or risky behaviors that could result in injury.
