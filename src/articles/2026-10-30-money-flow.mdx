This election cycle, UC employees donated over $1.7 million to campaigns in 47 different states and Washington, D.C.

The Daily Californian analyzed individual campaign contributions reported to the Federal Election Commission from Jan. 2025 to Sept. 2026 to see where UC employees are spending their political dollars.

Races in California received the most support from UC employees at $559,523 from 1,800 contributions. New York, Michigan, Illinois, Pennsylvania, and New Jersey trailed farther behind receiving from $63,000 to over $127,000 per state, but were the top five out-of-state recipients of campaign donations.

Rep. Ro Khanna, who is running for reelection as California’s 17th Congressional District representative, has raised $52,335 from UC donors. Closely behind is Rep. David Min, who received $50,631 for his reelection campaign to represent California’s 47th Congressional District.

New York campaigns raised the most money from UC donors except for California, pulling in $127,030 across 1,455 different contributions. A large share of those contributions – $43,756 – went to Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez’s reelection campaign for New York’s 14th Congressional District.

Just behind New York, donors sent $116,226 to Michigan campaigns across 383 different contributions. Abdul El-Sayed’s Senate campaign received the highest amount at $19,403. Running for Michigan’s 10th Congressional District, Eric Chung received $16,775. Additionally, Kristen McDonald Rivet, running to represent Michigan’s 8th Congressional District, has received $15,450.

On top of funds donated to campaign committees, UC employees donated over $2 million to political action committees, or PACs. A majority of these contributions went to hybrid and super PACs. Certain PACs received donations of hundreds of thousands of dollars.

Public First, a super PAC backed by Anthropic’s Dario Amodei promoting AI regulation and transparency, received a single $500,000 donation from a campus post-doctoral researcher, Michael Cohen. According to FEC records, it also appears that a Michael Cohen employed by UC Berkeley has also donated several thousand in contributions to Democrat Scott Wiener’s campaign for California’s 11th Congressional District as well as Republican Tom Cotton’s Senate reelection campaign in Arkansas.
