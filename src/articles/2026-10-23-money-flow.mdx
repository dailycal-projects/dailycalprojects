This election cycle, UC employees donated over $2 million to campaigns in 48 different states and Washington, D.C.

The Daily Californian analyzed individual campaign contributions reported to the Federal Election Commission from Jan. 2025 to Sept. 2026 to see where UC employees are spending their political dollars.

<MoneyFlowMap />

Races in California received the most support from UC employees at over $596,000 in contributions. Michigan, New York, Pennsylvania, Illinois, and New Jersey trailed farther behind at around $80,000 to over $143,000 per state, but were the top five recipients of campaign donations out of the rest of the country.

Rep. Ro Khanna, who is running for reelection as California’s 17th Congressional District representative, has raised $59,335 from UC donors. Closely behind is Rep. David Min, who received $57,431 for his reelection campaign to represent California’s 47th Congressional District.

Michigan raised the most money from UC donors except for California, pulling in $143,291 across 453 different contributions. Campaigns receiving the most amount of donation money include Rep. Kristen McDonald Rivet, running to represent Michigan's 8th Congressional District, at $22,450. Running for Michigan's 4th Congressional District, state Sen. Sean McCann received $21,492. Additionally, Abdul El-Sayed’s Senate campaign has received $20,118.

Just behind Michigan, donors sent $138,783 to New York campaigns across 1,608 different contributions. A large share of those contributions – $47,259 – went to Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez’s reelection campaign for New York’s 14th Congressional District.

On top of funds donated to campaign committees, UC employees further donated $2.7 million to political action committees, or PACs. A majority of these contributions went to hybrid and super PACs. Certain PACs have received donations of hundreds of thousands of dollars.

<PacBubbles />

UC employees have donated substantial amounts of money to both campaigns and PACs. Scott Shenker, a principal scientist for the campus-affiliated International Computer Science Institute and former Electrical Engineering & Computer Science professor, has donated over $1.2 million total to campaign committees and PACs in-state and across the country.

Shenker’s contributions included a single $160,000 donation to Everyday People, a Bay Area-based super PAC supporting progressive candidates and campaigns, as well as several other $100,000 donations to PACs such as Join the Union and the Democracy Defenders PAC. Shenker also donated about $150,000 in four separate contributions to the Working Class Heroes Fund, a hybrid PAC focused on organizing working-class voters and funding working-class candidate campaigns.

Public First, a super PAC backed by Anthropic’s Dario Amodei promoting AI regulation and transparency, received a single $500,000 donation from a campus post-doctoral researcher, Michael Cohen. According to FEC records, it also appears that Cohen has donated several thousand in contributions to Democrat Scott Wiener’s campaign for California’s 11th Congressional District as well as Republican Tom Cotton’s Senate reelection campaign in Arkansas.
